## Shrishti and Jugal

If you are looking for someone to ‘take’ your wedding pictures ‘for’ you, there are a bunch of people out there. If however, you want someone to ‘create’ your wedding pictures ‘with’ you, Gaatha is the team you should contact.
The thing that makes Gaatha stand out is their work ethic and attention to detail. They will deliver what they promised, and when they promised it. In our case, they delivered around 100 pictures within 10 days of the wedding. And this was during the peak Indian wedding season, so it is not a minor feat to deliver such high quality work on such a tight deadline.

Needless to say, the photographs were amazing, but that is not what this review is about. Their online portfolio and numerous awards reaffirm their skills as wedding photographers/cinematographers. It is the experience that I wanted to talk about. To quote my partner “And the photographs aren’t even the best part, it’s the experience we had with you all that I am gonna cherish the most”.

## About us  (`https://frozenvibes.in/about/`)

Our team brings together diverse backgrounds in photography, engineering, and visual arts, allowing us to offer a unique perspective on every wedding we shoot. We believe that every couple has a one-of-a-kind story, and our goal is to capture it in its most authentic form—full of joy, laughter, tears, and love.

Our work is about creating a comfortable environment where couples can be themselves, ensuring that each shot is genuine and reflective of your love.

With a commitment to quality, attention to detail, and a personal touch, we strive to make your wedding day unforgettable. Whether you’re planning a grand celebration or an intimate gathering, we’re here to preserve those cherished moments for you to relive time and time again.
